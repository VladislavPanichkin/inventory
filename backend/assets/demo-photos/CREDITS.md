(`src/modules/demo/demo-data.ts`).
All come from Wikimedia Commons under free licenses. Each was resized (full size up to
1280 px, thumbnail up to 600 px) and re-encoded as JPEG; nothing else was changed.
Where a license requires attribution, the credit is also shown in the app, in the
description of the sample item.
